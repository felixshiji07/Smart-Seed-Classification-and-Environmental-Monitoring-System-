# Smart-Seed-Classification-and-Environmental-Monitoring-System-
Seed quality assessment and environmental monitoring are essential for crop productivity, yet traditional methods are manual, slow, and prone to error. 
Seed quality assessment and environmental monitoring are essential components of modern precision agriculture. Traditional inspection methods are often labor-intensive and prone to human error, motivating the adoption of automated, AI-driven solutions. Recent research has highlighted the effectiveness of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and computer vision techniques for seed classification. Building upon these advances, this project proposes an integrated system using the ESP32-CAM for image acquisition, Python with OpenCV for preprocessing, and CNN models for seed type, size, and defect detection. Environmental parameters such as soil moisture, humidity, and temperature are measured using a soil moisture sensor and DHT22 sensor, and compared against seed-specific growth requirements. The combination of embedded AI and IoT sensors enables real-time monitoring and decision-making, aligning with prior work on embedded agricultural systems. The proposed system offers a low-cost, scalable, and portable solution that enhances seed classification accuracy while supporting smart farming practices. This approach demonstrates significant potential for sustainable agriculture by improving efficiency, reducing manual intervention, and enabling automated environmental control.
The rapid growth of artificial intelligence and embedded systems has transformed traditional agricultural practices into data driven, automated processes. Among these, seed classification and environmental monitoring are critical for ensuring crop quality and productivity. Seeds are the foundation of agriculture, and their accurate identification, size measurement, and defect detection directly influence germination success and yield. Conventional methods of seed inspection, which rely on manual observation or laboratory analysis, are often inefficient, costly, and unsuitable for large scale deployment.
The methodology adopted for this project integrates computer vision, deep learning, and embedded IoT systems to achieve automated seed classification and smart environmental monitoring. The process is divided into sequential stages to ensure accuracy and efficiency.
